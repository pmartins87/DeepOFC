## Question tested

M5J had shown that increasing the learned-response training budget from 256 to 1,024 iterations did not change the held-out deviation values.  M5I falls back to a uniform policy whenever a held-out visible-information key was not seen during response training.  This diagnostic measured how often held-out response decisions actually hit a trained exact key.

## Result

Across 8 state/player/budget rows there were 20,480 held-out response decision visits.  Only **3 visits** hit an exact response-training key; **20,477 visits used the fallback**.

Aggregate exact-key visit hit rate:

- `3 / 20,480 = 0.000146484375`
- approximately **0.01465%**.

The best individual row reached only `0.00078125` = **0.078125%** visit coverage.  Six of the eight rows had exactly zero trained-key hits.

At response budget 256:

- 10,240 held-out response decisions;
- 1 trained-key hit;
- aggregate visit hit rate ≈ **0.00977%**.

At response budget 1,024:

- 10,240 held-out response decisions;
- 2 trained-key hits;
- aggregate visit hit rate ≈ **0.01953%**.

Thus quadrupling response training increased the number of learned exact infosets substantially but left held-out exact-key coverage effectively zero.

## Consequence

The previous M5I/M5J held-out response numbers cannot be interpreted as measurements of a well-trained learned response over held-out play.  Almost every response action was generated by the uniform unseen-key fallback.

This does **not** make the earlier positive lower-bound deviations false: a profitable deviation discovered under such a weak response is still useful fail-fast evidence against the candidate.  It does mean that failure to find a larger deviation, or lack of improvement when scaling the M5I response budget, carries very little strategic information.

The correct next instrument is M5M: distill the response into the same bounded visible-information state/action generalizer used elsewhere, then evaluate candidate and generalized response on paired held-out deals and paired policy-uniform streams with explicit uncertainty on response-minus-candidate differences.
